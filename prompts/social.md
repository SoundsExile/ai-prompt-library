# Social Media Templates

Ready-to-post content for Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram, and TikTok.

---

**Prompt:** Write a tweet (max 280 characters) announcing `[product/service]`. Include: hook, key value, CTA. Tone: `[professional/witty/exciting]`. Add 2-3 relevant hashtags.

**Context:** Twitter/X announcement
**Output:** Punchy, character-limited, engagement-focused

---

**Prompt:** Create a LinkedIn post (200-300 words) sharing a case study about `[client]` who achieved `[specific result]` using `[product]`. Include: challenge, solution, measurable outcome, and a quote.

**Context:** LinkedIn case study
**Output:** Professional narrative, metrics-driven

---

**Prompt:** Write an Instagram caption (125 characters max) for `[image/video description]`. Include: relevant emoji, 3-5 hashtags (mix of broad + niche).

**Context:** Instagram post
**Output:** Short, visual-focused, hashtag-optimized

---

**Prompt:** Write a LinkedIn article intro (150 words) on the topic of `[industry trend]`. Hook readers in first 2 sentences, provide one surprising insight, end with a question to drive comments.

**Context:** LinkedIn long-form
**Output:** Thought leadership opening

---

**Prompt:** Create 5 TikTok video ideas (one sentence each) for promoting `[product/service]`. Each should be: visual/action-oriented, trending-format adaptable, 15-60 seconds actionable.

**Context:** TikTok content planning
**Output:** Creative, trend-aware concepts

---

**Prompt:** Write a Facebook ad primary text (125 characters) and headline (25 characters) for `[offer]`. Target audience: `[demographic]`. Include urgency and clear CTA.

**Context:** Facebook advertising
**Output:** High-converting ad copy

---

**Prompt:** Create a thread of 5 tweets on `[topic]`. Each tweet: build on previous, include 1-2 insights or tips, end with a hook for the next tweet. Total thread should be informative and flow naturally.

**Context:** Twitter thread
**Output:** Serialized content, engagement loop

---

**Prompt:** Write an Instagram carousel caption for `[educational content]`. Include: hook slide text, 8-10 content slides descriptions, and a CTA for the final slide.

**Context:** Instagram carousel
**Output:** Slide-by-slide structure

---

**Prompt:** Create a LinkedIn poll with 4 options on `[controversial question]`. Include: provocative question, clear options, and relevant industry tags.

**Context:** LinkedIn engagement
**Output:** Poll setup with engagement potential

---

**Prompt:** Write a "keystone content" tweet: one tweet that encapsulates your core philosophy on `[topic]`. Should be memorable, quotable, and represent your brand voice. Max 280 characters.

**Context:** Brand positioning
**Output:** Iconic, shareable, on-brand
