# Product Descriptions

E-commerce copy that sells. Adapt each template to your product.

---

**Prompt:** Write a compelling 100-word product description for `[product name]`, a `[category]` targeting `[audience]`. Focus on the top 3 benefits, include sensory language, and end with a subtle call-to-action.

**Context:** Luxury or premium positioning
**Output:** Persuasive paragraphs, benefit-driven, emotional appeal

---

**Prompt:** Create five SEO-optimized product bullet points for `[product name]`. Each should be under 20 words, start with a benefit, and naturally incorporate keywords: `[keyword 1]`, `[keyword 2]`, `[keyword 3]`.

**Context:** Amazon or e-commerce listing
**Output:** 5 bullet points, keyword-rich, scannable

---

**Prompt:** Write a short (50-word) product description for `[product name]` in a `[tone]` tone. Highlight `[unique selling point]` and why it beats competitors.

**Context:** Quick promotional copy
**Output:** Concise, punchy, high-converting

---

**Prompt:** Create a "day in the life" product description for `[product name]` that shows how `[target audience]` uses it from morning to night. Make it relatable and aspirational.

**Context:** Lifestyle brand positioning
**Output:** Narrative flow, relatable scenarios, emotional hook

---

**Prompt:** Write three variations of a product description for `[product name]`:
1. Feature-focused (technical audience)
2. Benefit-focused (mass market)
3. Story-focused (premium brand)

**Context:** A/B testing or multi-channel needs
**Output:** Three distinct versions, ~75 words each

---

**Prompt:** Create a comparison-style product description positioning `[product name]` against `[competitor]`. Use a table format showing features on the left, your product advantages on the right.

**Context:** Landing page or sales enablement
**Output:** Table format, clear differentiation

---

**Prompt:** Write SEO product descriptions for a category page featuring `[product category]`. Create one introduction paragraph and 3-4 individual product descriptions (50 words each). Include `[long-tail keyword]`.

**Context:** Category page optimization
**Output:** Hierarchical copy, keyword-optimized

---

**Prompt:** Craft a product description for `[product name]` written from the perspective of a satisfied customer. Use first-person, include specific results, and mention one minor hesitation we overcame.

**Context:** Testimonial-style copy
**Output:** Authentic voice, relatable, trustworthy
