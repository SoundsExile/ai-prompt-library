# Email Outreach Templates

Cold emails and follow-ups that get responses. Customize bracketed items.

---

**Prompt:** Write a cold outreach email (under 150 words) to `[target persona]` introducing `[your product/service]`. Lead with a relevant insight or pain point, demonstrate brief understanding of their business, and include a soft call-to-action.

**Context:** Initial cold outreach
**Output:** Concise, personalized, value-first

---

**Prompt:** Create a follow-up email for `[prospect name]` who didn't respond to the first email about `[product]`. Reference the original briefly, add one new piece of social proof or case study, and end with a low-commitment question.

**Context:** Follow-up sequence
**Output:** Non-pushy, adds value, easy response

---

**Prompt:** Write a 3-email sequence for a `[industry]` prospect interested in `[product]`. Email 1: Value proposition with relevant insight. Email 2: Case study from similar client. Email 3: Last chance / calendar invite.

**Context:** Automated outreach sequence
**Output:** 3 distinct emails, progressive commitment

---

**Prompt:** Craft a re-engagement email for inactive subscribers/users. Acknowledge it's been a while, share one major update or improvement to `[product]`, and offer `[specific incentive]`.

**Context:** Re-engagement campaigns
**Output:** Friendly, low-pressure, update-focused

---

**Prompt:** Write a warm introduction email connecting `[person A]` and `[person B]`. Include why the connection is valuable for both, keep under 100 words, and suggest a concrete next step.

**Context:** Networking intros
**Output:** Brief, value-add, actionable

---

**Prompt:** Create a webinar invitation email for `[topic]`. Include: compelling hook, date/time, key takeaways (3 bullets), and registration link. Tone: `[professional/casual]`.

**Context:** Event promotion
**Output:** Conversion-focused, clear CTA

---

**Prompt:** Write an email announcing `[product update/feature]`. Use the "PSS" structure: Problem (they had), Solution (what we built), Story (quick customer win). Keep it under 200 words.

**Context:** Product update announcement
**Output:** Narrative-driven, benefit-focused

---

**Prompt:** Craft a "break-up" email for customers who cancelled `[product]`. Thank them for being a customer, ask one quick feedback question, and leave the door open for future return.

**Context:** Cancellation recovery
**Output:** Gracious, learning-oriented, non-pushy

---

**Prompt:** Write a referral request email to `[customer name]`. Reference their recent success with `[product]`, explain our referral program benefits, and make asking easy with a pre-written message they can forward.

**Context:** Referral programs
**Output:** Complimentary, easy action, incentivized

---

**Prompt:** Create a post-purchase thank you email for `[product]`. Include: genuine gratitude, quick getting-started tip, invite to community/Social, and soft upsell for accessories/supplements.

**Context:** Post-purchase sequence
**Output:** Warm, educational, relationship-building
